FireBoy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

FireBoy (package app.fegba, "the App") is a Game Boy Advance emulator for Android. We respect your privacy. The App does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

We have no user accounts and no servers that receive your data. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

2. Games and ROMs

The App does not provide, distribute or include any copyrighted game ROMs. Users are responsible for obtaining and using game files lawfully.

3. Data stored on your device

  • Settings, control layouts, save states, battery saves, screenshots and ROM library metadata are stored locally in the App's private storage (or in folders you choose).
  • ROM files are never uploaded. The App accesses only the files and folders you explicitly select through the Android system file picker. The App does not provide or distribute any games.
  • You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

4. Network access

The App requests the INTERNET permission only to (a) download game cover images and (b) download the machine-learning model used for image processing (depth effect). Cover images are fetched from public hosts such as GitHub (libretro-thumbnails), jsDelivr, TheGamesDB, an image proxy (wsrv.nl) and web image search, and the model from Hugging Face or a mirror. To find a cover, the game's title (derived from the file name) may be sent in the request. No ROM content, account or device identifiers are sent by the App; these hosts may see standard connection data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

5. Permissions

  • INTERNET – downloading cover images and the image-processing model.
  • VIBRATE – haptic feedback for virtual buttons.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below only) – saving files to storage you choose.

6. Google Play purchases

The App offers in-app purchases. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google may process payment-related information (such as your payment method, billing details and purchase history) under Google's Privacy Policy. The App itself never accesses or stores your bank card or other payment details; it only receives the purchase status needed to unlock paid features.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no data from anyone.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions: wangqifreeze@gmail.com

FireBoy 隐私权政策

最后更新:2026-10-12

FireBoy(包名 app.fegba,以下简称"本应用")是一款 Android 平台的 Game Boy Advance 模拟器。本应用不会收集、上传、共享或出售任何个人信息。

1. 我们不收集的信息

本应用没有用户账号,也没有接收用户数据的服务器;不使用广告或第三方统计分析 SDK。

2. 游戏与 ROM

本应用不提供、不分发、不包含任何版权游戏 ROM,用户负责合法获取和使用游戏文件。

3. 存储在您设备上的数据

  • 设置、按键布局、即时存档、电池存档、截图及游戏库元数据仅保存在本应用的私有存储(或您自行选择的文件夹)中。
  • ROM 文件不会被上传。本应用仅访问您通过 Android 系统文件选择器明确选择的文件和文件夹。本应用不提供也不分发任何游戏。
  • 您可随时通过清除应用数据或卸载应用来删除这些数据。

4. 网络访问

本应用申请 INTERNET 权限,仅用于 (a) 下载游戏封面图片,以及 (b) 下载图像处理(景深效果)所用的机器学习模型。封面来自 GitHub(libretro-thumbnails)、jsDelivr、TheGamesDB、图片代理(wsrv.nl)及网络图片搜索等公开站点,模型来自 Hugging Face 或其镜像。查找封面时,可能会在请求中包含游戏名称(源自文件名)。本应用不会发送 ROM 内容、账号或设备标识符;上述站点可能依其自身隐私政策获取 IP 地址等标准连接信息。

5. 权限说明

  • INTERNET – 下载封面图片和图像处理模型。
  • VIBRATE – 虚拟按键的震动反馈。
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE(仅 Android 9 及以下)– 将文件保存到您选择的存储位置。

6. Google Play 购买

本应用提供应用内购买。购买通过 Google Play Billing 处理。Google 可能依其隐私权政策处理与付款相关的信息(如支付方式、账单信息和购买记录)。本应用本身不会接触或存储您的银行卡或其他支付信息,仅获得解锁付费功能所需的购买状态。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童,也不会有意收集任何人的数据。

8. 政策变更

我们可能更新本政策,更新内容将发布于本页面并更改日期。
